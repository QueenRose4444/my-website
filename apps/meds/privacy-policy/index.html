Medication & Weight Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The Medication & Weight Tracker at rosestuffs.org/meds/ is a web app for recording the medication you take, your weight and, if you switch it on, pain. It runs in your browser. It is a personal project run by one developer, not a health service.

Without an account, everything you enter stays in your browser and none of it is sent to us. If you create an account, your entries are stored on our server so that your devices can share them. They are health information, and they are not end-to-end encrypted: the developer is technically able to read them. There is no advertising and no analytics script, and nothing you enter is sold or passed to anyone else.

What leaves your device

1. Loading the page. The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and your browser's details when a page loads, and it adds a small script of its own that checks whether a visitor is an automated program. The page's typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and the address of the page you are on, as with any web font. Google receives nothing that you enter.

2. An account, if you make one. An account is optional and is only a username and a password. This app does not ask for an email address. That also means there is no way to recover a forgotten password.

  • Your password is not sent. Your browser turns it into a different secret and sends that, and the server keeps only a salted hash of what it receives. The one exception is an account made before this scheme existed: it sends its password a single time, on the sign-in that moves it over.
  • Sign-in and registration attempts are counted per username and per IP address, so that guessing can be blocked. The server keeps that IP address beside its counter.

3. Your entries, if you are signed in. Everything you record is sent to the sync service and stored under your account: your medications, each dose and its time, injection sites, supply, weights, pain entries, notes and settings. It travels over HTTPS and is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure in a form the developer could read. A random id for each browser goes with it, so that changes made on two devices can be told apart.

The service also keeps a history of your changes, at present without a time limit, so that a mistake or a bad sync can be undone. An entry you delete in the app is removed from your current data but stays in that history on the server. The same is true of “Reset all data”. To have the history erased, see Deleting your data.

4. Reminders, if you switch notifications on. Reminders need an account. When you switch them on:

  • Your browser creates a push subscription, which is an address at the push service run by your browser's maker (Google, Mozilla or Apple) together with encryption keys. It is stored by our notifications service until you switch notifications off.
  • The app uploads about the next week of reminders: the time of each one and its text. The text includes the medication's name and the dose, and the injection site if you track that. Reminders are replaced whenever your schedule changes and are deleted a week after their time.
  • Each reminder is delivered through that push service, encrypted so that the push service cannot read its text.
  • If you answer a reminder from the notification itself, for example by asking to be reminded again, that answer is sent to the notifications service so it can act on it.

A reminder shows a medication's name on your screen. Depending on your phone's settings that can include the lock screen, where someone else may see it.

5. Links you open. Medication information in the app links to public sources such as official product information. Nothing is sent to those sites unless you open a link.

What stays on your device

  • Your entries and settings, in this site's browser storage, along with your sign-in session if you have one. Without an account this is the only copy.
  • Files you download. A backup file holds everything you have recorded, and a spreadsheet holds the dates you chose. They stay wherever you saved them, and the app cannot delete them.

The app has no lock of its own. Anyone who can use your browser on your device can open the page and read what is there.

Permissions

  • Notifications, asked for only when you switch reminders on.

The app does not ask for your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: Settings, then “Reset all data”, or clear this site's data in your browser. Downloaded files are yours to delete.
  • Reminders: switching notifications off removes this browser's push subscription from our notifications service.
  • On our server: there is no delete-account button yet. Write to the address below and give the username, and the account will be deleted together with its entries, their history and any reminders. You may be asked to show that the account is yours, for example by changing something in it on request.

Backup copies of the database that the developer keeps against data loss can still hold deleted data until they are replaced by newer ones.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly hold information about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
