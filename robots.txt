# WIP pages are kept out of search results by:
#   1. a per-page  <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">  (works on
#      every host, including the dev site — this is the one that matters), and
#   2. the  /wip/*  X-Robots-Tag rule in  _headers  (Cloudflare Pages only).
#
# Do NOT add "Disallow: /wip/" back here. Blocking the crawl stops Google from
# ever fetching the page, so it never reads the noindex — and bare /wip/ URLs
# can then still show up in a "site:" search. Crawl must be allowed for the
# noindex to do its job.
